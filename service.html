Service

Service is one of my core passions, in both academia and my previous life as a student-athlete.

University and Departmental Leadership

University of Maryland

  • Graduate Student Professional Development Officer — Elected by fellow graduate students, I lead the Graduate Student Workshop Series in the Department of Government and Politics. Collaborated with the Director of Graduate Studies and the Graduate Student Association to organize regular sessions where faculty and alumni discussed issues related to professional development.
  • Methods Workshop Coordinator — Led the Graduate Student Methods Workshop and organized a guest lecture on using LLMs in political science research.
  • Summer Research Workshop Series — Founded and organized a summer research workshop series, offering seven students the opportunity to present their projects to other graduate students.

Towson University

  • Team Captain — Elected by my teammates, I served as team captain of Towson University's Division I Men's Swimming and Diving program for two consecutive seasons.
  • Student-Athlete Advisory Committee (SAAC) — The only four-year member of SAAC, which advised executive leadership in the Department of Athletics.
  • Total Tiger Award — Awarded during my senior year in recognition of my leadership and service.
